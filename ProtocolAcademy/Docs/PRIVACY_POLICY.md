# Privacy Policy — Protocol Academy

**Draft.** This describes what the app does as shipped in this repository. Host it at a
public URL, put that URL in `AppLinks.privacyPolicy` and in App Store Connect, and
update it if you add an ad network or any other SDK. It is not legal advice.

_Last updated: [DATE]_

## The short version

Protocol Academy does not ask who you are, does not create an account, and does not
send your learning activity anywhere. Your progress is stored on your device.

## What the app stores on your device

- Which lessons you have read and your best quiz score for each protocol.
- Whether you have opted in to personalised advertising.

This is kept in the app's local storage and is removed when you delete the app. You can
clear it at any time with **Settings ▸ Reset progress**. It is never uploaded.

## Purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple. The app asks the App Store what you own and
unlocks content accordingly. It never sees your payment details, your Apple Account
email, or your name. Apple's own privacy policy covers the transaction.

## Advertising

The free version of the app shows advertising.

- Ads are **non-personalised by default**. No advertising identifier is used and no
  tracking permission is requested.
- You may turn on personalised ads in **Settings ▸ Advertising**. Doing so asks for iOS
  tracking permission first, and you can withdraw it at any time in iOS Settings ▸
  Privacy & Security ▸ Tracking.
- Buying **Remove Ads** or **Academy Pro** removes all advertising permanently.

> If you link a third-party ad network, that network's data collection must be described
> here in specific terms — what it collects, what it is used for, and a link to its own
> privacy policy.

## Analytics

There are none. The app contains no analytics, crash-reporting, or attribution SDK.

## Children

The app is rated 12+ and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal
information from anyone, of any age.

## Your rights

Because no personal data leaves your device, there is nothing for us to export, correct,
or delete on your behalf. Deleting the app removes everything the app stored.
